# $UFFiHHiN ESG Impact Metrics Framework
Environmental claims are evidence objects, not self-certifying facts. Scope 1/2/3, energy, water, soil, biodiversity, materials, waste, logistics and toxicology measurements require a declared boundary, methodology, source provenance, uncertainty and independent verification. Signed telemetry proves provenance; it does not by itself prove carbon additionality, permanence, regulatory compliance, or offset eligibility. Verification states: VERIFIED, DISPROVED, PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, UNVERIFIABLE, CONFLICTED. Standards mappings remain mappings until independently assessed.
